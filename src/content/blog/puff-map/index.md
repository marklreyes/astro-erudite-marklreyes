A quick and easy [data visualization](https://www.puffprovisions.com/cannabis-map/) made for Puff Provisions LLC, a news and media company based out of Southern California.

This web asset illustrates the current state of Cannabis legalization throughout the USA.

This asset is powered by JavaScript charting library, D3.js.
