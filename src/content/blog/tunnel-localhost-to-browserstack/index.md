1. Sign in to, _[http://www.browserstack.com](http://www.browserstack.com)_
2. Click, **Web tunnel**.
3. Flag, **Local server**.
4. Input your **Host** and Port (i.e. **10.61.0.125** and **8888**).
5. Click, Finish.

**HINTS:** Use Firefox or Safari as Chrome is a 32-bit browser.
